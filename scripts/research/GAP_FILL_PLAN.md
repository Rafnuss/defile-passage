# Gap filling: final status of the five goals

Development paused on 12 October 2026 at the user's request. The single retained weather candidate and all main scores and limitations are in [MODEL_SPECIFICATION.md](MODEL_SPECIFICATION.md). Original production remains in use for historical season totals. No further tests are queued.

| Step | Original scientific goal                                   | Consolidated status                                                                                                                               | Unmet condition for production reconsideration                                                                                                     |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Correct assessment and reliable numerical fitting          | Matched masks, common weather classification, species/year/era review, paired block bootstrap and saved independent numerical checks implemented. | Independent confirmation beyond repeatedly reused development masks.                                                                               |
| 2    | Species-level calibration, especially rain                 | Selected hourly radiation/cloud model improves aggregate wet-gap point predictions; species and rain-band losses remain visible.                  | Severe-rain truth, historical dry accuracy and species-level calibration remain insufficient.                                                      |
| 3    | Within-day dependence and retained-observation information | Retained-hour adjustments and episode/daily-state alternatives assessed; no adjustment or daily state adopted. Daily-state branch closed.         | No validated local conditioning for historical partial days; local error gains did not deliver better seasonal reconstruction.                     |
| 4    | Predictive uncertainty and independent validation          | Earlier uncertainty experiments preserved as historical evidence.                                                                                 | The selected model has no calibrated joint predictive count distribution or hourly/day/season intervals. Earlier coverage results do not transfer. |
| 5    | Mixed temporal scales, history and production comparison   | Hourly means aggregate to intact survey totals; historical species scope and forty-context production comparison assessed.                        | Historical dry and reconstructed-season scores still lose to original. No production replacement.                                                  |

The selected model resolves the mean-aggregation design and keeps fitting small and fast. It does not complete all five scientific goals. Unmet conditions are documented limitations, not an active implementation plan.

The previous full plan, including abandoned architectures and superseded next steps, is preserved in [archive/DEVELOPMENT_HISTORY.md](archive/DEVELOPMENT_HISTORY.md). See [README.md](README.md) for code and evidence locations.
