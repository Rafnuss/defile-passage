## Assessment rules

Resample calendar-week blocks jointly across species and compute equal-species MAE differences within each bootstrap replicate. Averaging species interval endpoints is invalid. Daily wet/dry comparisons use one exposure-weighted mean hourly rain over matching held-out intervals for every model; wet means at least 0.1 mm/hour. Folds 0–2 are disjoint whole-day masks, fold 3 is a separate short-gap task, folds 5–8 hold out 2022–2025, and fold 4 is an all-recent-data reference rather than validation. Reused masks make these exploratory comparisons, not nested model selection.

## Numerical stabilization and pooling (12 October 2026)

Prior-centered quadrature produced 71 integration flags among 128 contexts. Posterior-centered log-state integration resolves these: both the adaptive and pooled batches have zero flags, checked at 31 versus 63 nodes. All pooled fits pass gradient/Hessian checks; maximum fitted gradient is 0.000914, directional derivative error 0.0000795, and refined integral gradient difference 0.000130. Stable forward means also pass 121/243-node comparisons; exact near-zero contributions and the Gamma shape-shift identity prevent low-shape/high-hazard inaccuracies. Flat retained-positive posterior draws use log-state CDF inversion, tested against grid refinement and independent integration.

The new positive penalty adds strength 24 times the exposure-weighted variance of the positive log-rate to the existing GAM penalty. It shrinks variation around the species baseline without penalizing its intercept. Strength 24 is a prespecified sensitivity candidate, not tuned on validation. The former tenfold smoothing and simpler positive-only candidates remain rejected.

Across whole-day masks at ≥3 mm/hour, adaptive predictions total 5,035 birds and pooled predictions 4,498 versus 1,390 observed. Passerines improve from 2,319 to 1,537 versus 1,070 observed, but priority-species excess remains. For 2025 Redwing, pooled mean daily 95% interval widths are about 27 birds on dry days and 36 on wet days, replacing the earlier multi-million-bird intervals. This fixes an important pathology, but coverage alone does not establish useful calibration.

On matched whole-day gaps, pooled minus production equal-species MAE is +4.65 birds on dry days (paired week-bootstrap interval −8.46 to +19.21) and −53.04 on wet days (−105.22 to −13.22). Short-gap differences overlap zero. Held-out-year pooled versus Gamma/flocks daily differences favor pooling in dry and wet groups, but performance varies by species/year and short dry gaps can worsen. No universal production replacement is selected.

## Mixed-scale scope and assumptions

Counts are either observed hourly counts or one intact species-survey total. Do not split unresolved observed totals or duplicate their hourly counterparts. Actual coverage fractions retain zeros in fully observed hours, preserve civil-daylight coordinates outside [0,1], and align hourly weather with UTC clock hours. The accepted recent inputs have no repeated species-clock-hour components. Independent-interval treatment is therefore appropriate for these inputs; future overlapping subdivisions require a joint multinomial allocation likelihood.

The canonical full-hour hurdle NB law retains one size parameter within that hour. Partial coverage binomially thins this count, and longer observations sum their covered hourly counts. The fitter integrates one shared Gamma state over the product of all observations on a date. Small coarse counts use exact log-domain convolution; larger totals use exponential tilting and alias-free finite FFT convolution, checked against direct convolution, derivatives and rare/extreme-rate cases. Positive survey totals are never allocated as observed hourly counts.

The first coherent refit estimates four calibration coefficients: gate/count intercepts and rain slopes, with a standard-normal penalty. Training GAM shapes, daily Gamma shape and NB size remain fixed. Its predictive intervals include those four coefficients, daily states and count variation, but exclude uncertainty in the frozen components. They must not be presented as full model uncertainty or compared favorably merely because they are narrower. Full-reference fits use all recent data and are not validation.

The user's systematic passerine recording confirmation applies only to complete surveys in 2022–2025. Priority 2022 protocol coverage remains assumed pending the missing report. Closures and missing observation effort remain unknown. The 43,489 recorded historical species-survey totals are an inventory with unverified scope, not eligible training data; absent historical entries cannot become zeros without further evidence.
